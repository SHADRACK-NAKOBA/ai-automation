## Executive Summary

On 2025-06-10, the production payment service (`payment-service-prod`) experienced three JVM OutOfMemoryError crashes caused by a heap memory leak introduced in release v2.4.1, resulting in approximately 12–15 minutes of cumulative payment processing downtime across a 65-minute incident window. Each restart triggered a 4–5 minute service outage during which customers were unable to complete payment transactions, affecting an estimated 1,500 unique customers across the three restart events. The incident was mitigated by doubling the JVM heap allocation from 2 GB to 4 GB, which suppressed further restarts while a permanent code-level fix is scheduled for a future release.

## Root Cause
